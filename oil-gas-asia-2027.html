About the Event

Where South Asia's Energy Sector Sets Policy and Strikes Deals

The Global Energy & Renewables Forum brings together oil and gas operators, power utilities, and renewable energy developers with the government policymakers who regulate them - covering grid modernization, solar and wind development, and upstream oil and gas exploration.

Held in Islamabad specifically to keep policymakers and regulators in the room, past editions have directly informed energy sector policy discussions at the federal level.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is entry free for visitors?

Entry is free for pre-registered trade visitors. On-site registration is also available, subject to capacity.

How do I book an exhibitor stall?

Contact our exhibitor relations team at events@badarexpo.com or call +92-21-3482-1159. Stall packages start at 9 sqm and include standard shell scheme fabrication.

Is parking available at the venue?

Yes, the Pak-China Friendship Centre has dedicated on-site parking, with additional overflow parking arranged during peak hours.

Who do I contact for sponsorship opportunities?

Reach our partnerships team at events@badarexpo.com. Platinum, Gold, and Official Partner tiers are currently available for this edition.
