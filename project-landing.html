International Project

China Trade Delegation Partnership

A multi-day trade delegation program connecting Chinese manufacturers with Pakistani importers and distributors, organized in partnership with the Chinese Embassy in Karachi.

View Related Work Contact Us
Project Overview

Opening a Direct Channel Between Two Markets

The Chinese Embassy in Karachi approached BXSS to design and host a structured trade delegation program - not a public exhibition, but a closed-door series of meetings between a visiting Chinese manufacturing delegation and pre-qualified Pakistani importers across textiles, machinery, and electronics.

BXSS managed the full engagement: venue selection, delegation logistics, interpreter coordination, and a matchmaking schedule that turned a one-day courtesy visit into three days of structured B2B meetings.

What BXSS Delivered

Objectives

What the Program Set Out to Achieve

  1. Open Direct Dialogue

    Connect Chinese manufacturers with Pakistani buyers without a middleman layer.

  2. Qualify the Right Buyers

    Pre-screen local importers so delegation time was spent on serious, relevant meetings.

  3. Document the Partnership

    Produce press-ready coverage supporting both the embassy's and BXSS's public record.

Challenges

What Made This Program Complex

Language & Cultural Coordination

Every meeting needed accurate interpretation and culturally aware scheduling.

Diplomatic Security Protocols

Embassy-level security requirements shaped venue choice and access control.

Compressed Timeline

The full program had to be planned and confirmed in under three weeks.

Solutions

How BXSS Responded

Dedicated Interpreter Team

Professional Mandarin-English interpreters assigned to every meeting slot.

Secure Venue Partnership

Coordinated directly with venue security and local authorities in advance.

Parallel-Track Planning

Logistics, buyer vetting, and press coordination run simultaneously, not sequentially.
