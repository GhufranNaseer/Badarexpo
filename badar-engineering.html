Our Engineering Legacy

Built on Six Decades of Technical Expertise

Since 1965, Badar Engineering Company (Pvt) Ltd has built a trusted reputation in engineering, technical contracting and industrial infrastructure across Pakistan.

From civil construction and MEP systems to power distribution, industrial automation, structural steel and turnkey EPC execution, our engineering capabilities have evolved alongside the demands of modern industry.

Our legacy is defined by technical discipline, dependable execution and a commitment to building infrastructure that performs for decades.
